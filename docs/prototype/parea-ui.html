Parea · interface direction

A plan
is a ticket.

You punch it going in. You keep it after.

Group chats are where plans go to die. Parea turns the "we should do something" message into an object you can hold: a stub with a date, a spot, and a hole punched through it when you commit. Then the night happens, and the stub comes back with a photo on it. Nineteen screens — squads as real profiles that talk to each other, plans, RSVPs, the map, the thread, and a calendar of everything you actually showed up to.

Whitewash and Aegean cobalt. Sun yellow does one job only — it rings the punch hole when you're in.
Type
Alegreya Sans 800 for anything you read at a glance, Commissioner for interface text, DM Mono for ticket codes, times and distances. All three carry Greek.
Shape
Perforated cards, 20px corners, one floating glass tab bar. No gradients, no accent rails.
Squads
A squad is a profile, not a chat room: handle, bio, members, a wall of nights. Two squads follow each other and throw one night together.
Rooms
A plan can open a room that lives 48 hours — one day before the night, one day after. That is the only place two pareas talk, and it is the only thread with a clock on it.
After
A plan that has passed keeps a roll of photos, added by the people who were actually in it, and those covers surface on the week strip. Screens marked Built exist in the app; Not yet is where this is heading.
Scroll
Every screen below scrolls inside its own frame. Try it.
